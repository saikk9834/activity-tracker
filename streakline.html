You're not overweight — you're under-muscled with fat stored at the belly. The goal is recomposition: losing fat and building muscle at the same time, which beginners can absolutely do with a mild deficit and high protein. You cannot spot-reduce belly fat: it leaves when overall body fat drops, and it's the last place to go. Expect the waist to move around weeks 8–12, not week 3.

Consistency

The five mechanics that keep you going

Shrink the promise, keep the frequency

Your commitment is "I go to the YMCA on my scheduled days" — not 30 minutes, not a specific workout. On a terrible day, 10 minutes counts as a win. You're training the habit of going; intensity is easy to add to a habit that exists.

Decide once, in the calendar

Vague plans fail; scheduled ones succeed. The sessions live in your calendar like standup meetings. You never decide whether — that decision is already made.

Stack the habit on your work shutdown

Close laptop → gym bag already packed by the door → leave immediately. The couch gap between work and workout is where the habit dies — eliminate it.

Never miss twice

Missing one day is noise. Missing two in a row is the start of a new (bad) habit. A missed day must be followed by an attended day, even a 15-minute one.

Track appearances, not outcomes

Your metric for the first 12 weeks is X's per month (aim for 24+ here), not kilograms. Weigh twice a week, judge only the 7-day average — daily ±1 kg swings are water, not fat.

Lifting

The progression rule

This is the entire "how much weight" answer, forever — double progression:

  • Each exercise has a rep range (e.g. 8–12). When you complete all sets at the top of the range with honest form (~2 reps left in the tank), add weight next session: +2.5 kg upper body, +5 kg leg press and machines, next dumbbell up.
  • The new weight drops you back to ~8 reps. Climb back to 12. Repeat.
  • If you can't beat your logbook two sessions in a row, the weight stays. That's normal.

Effort dial across the 12 weeks

Weeks 1–2Stop 3–4 reps short of failure — calibrate, learn form, log every weight
Weeks 3–6Stop 2–3 reps short
Weeks 7–12Stop 1–2 reps short on the last set of each exercise

Warm-up before every lift: 5 min brisk incline walk, then one light set (~50%) of the first two exercises. The starting weights in this app are first guesses for an untrained 82 kg man — week 1's job is to correct them and tap the chips to save yours.

Swimming

Swim days, decoded

A length = one end of the pool to the other, once (~30–45 s for a beginner). A round = a swim plus the rest after it. Effort /10: 4–5 = relaxed, could chat; 7 = breathing hard but recovered in ~30 s; 9 = near max — never required.

All your swim time is broken into short pieces with rest — ten one-length swims with rests gives more quality work than ten minutes of sloppy continuous struggle. That's why these sessions are doable even though "swim 30 minutes straight" wasn't.

  • Tuesday — the workout swim. Hard on purpose; this is the fat-loss engine. Recovered before the rest ends? Swim harder. Rest not enough? Ease off a notch.
  • Thursday — the recovery swim. Deliberately the easiest session of the week. No counting, no targets — don't turn it into a second workout; Friday needs fresh legs.
  • Saturday — the pyramid. Climb 1-2-3-4 lengths and back down at a steady 6–7/10. The peak quietly grows every 2 weeks, and in a few months "20+ lengths straight" falls out of it naturally.

Scaling any swim: too hard → rest longer or cut rounds (never swim sloppy); too easy → shorten rests first, then add rounds. Terrible day → 6 easy lengths and out. The X still counts.

The target

130–150 g protein · ~2,250 kcal · every day

Muscle-building benefit plateaus around 1.6–1.8 g/kg — that's 130–148 g at 82 kg. 130 g is the floor, 150 g is a great day. Vegetarian protein arrives with carbs or fat attached, so the structure below matters more than heroics: 30–40 g protein per meal, four times a day, each meal anchored by a Tier 1 food.

Desk-worker rules

The guardrails

  • Protein first at each meal — most satiating macro, kills the 4 pm snack drift.
  • Liquid calories are the belly-fat budget. Sugary drinks, juices, fancy coffees, alcohol — the easiest 300–500 kcal to delete. Beer is a double hit: calories + worse sleep, and poor sleep increases belly-fat storage.
  • Don't drink the deficit back after swimming. A 30-min swim burns ~250–350 kcal and spikes hunger; have yogurt or a shake ready.
  • Fight the chair. 10-min walks after lunch and dinner, pace during calls — worth 150–300 kcal/day, as much as the swim.
  • Weekends count. Keep the breakfast + protein structure; loosen dinner, not the whole day.
  • Adjust by the trend: target 0.25–0.5 kg/week down on the 7-day average. Faster → add ~150 kcal. Flat 2+ weeks and waist stuck → cut ~150 kcal. Scale flat but waist shrinking → recomposition working, change nothing.
  • B12 is covered by dairy. Mind iron (dal, rajma, spinach + lemon/tomato for absorption).
  • Creatine monohydrate 3–5 g/day — vegetarian, cheap, best-studied supplement there is, and vegetarians see the biggest benefit. If you buy one thing besides whey, it's this.
